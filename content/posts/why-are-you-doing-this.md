## Why are you doing this????
I'd always wanted to do a Masters. In my final year of my Bachelors, I mentioned this to my project supervisor but he warned me against it. He was in his older years and I got the impression that it was a decision he had regretted. So I took his advice and decided to get a job instead.

It was a different world back in 1997. The internet was still mostly confined to academia (it's original intended purpose), Netscape browser and Yahoo search engine existed but there was no e-Commerce at all - no Amazon, no YouTube, no TikTok, no Instagram, no Netflix. Apple was in crisis and had lost £1bn as Microsoft started to eat into its market share. The only mobile phones were the size of a brick and email was only just begining to take off. 

However, there was a real sense of possibility, and if you had a background in computing it was really easy to get a job. So easy in fact that the tables were turned in the interview process..... you were interviewing the employer, not the other way around! In fact, 2 years after I graduated, I was introduced to one employer in the pub by a friend who had vouched for me. Although I wasn't even looking to switch jobs he interviewed me there and then. I'm not joking when I say the interview process was "How many stock options do you want?" Thats what it was like! (I didn't take the job as I felt the guy was a bit of a cowboy - his company went bust a few years later, but he gave it a good run!)

I ended up working for a small US Consultancy with a base in Dublin. Many of the employees were under 25 years old - we had a great time - fresh out of college, getting paid well, no student debt (there were no fees) and absolutely zero commitments! I was lucky enough to get posted with 2 others to a role in Silicon Valley - what a great time, we couldn;t believe our luck! How things have changed....

Anyway, I worked there for 3 years, then did 5 years at an eCommerce software company - travelling aournd Europe to help customers implement their eCommerce websites. Eventually I joined one of the big Consultancies (the kind that advertise in airports and sponsor golf events) and spent the next 20 years getting well paid to solve problems (it's not just  Powerpoint and Excel you know!).

But the niggle to do a Masters never quite went away.

Recently my role became more of a manager rather than a doer. I was moving further and further away from technology and beginning to get frustrated and bored. I hadn't learned anything new or interesting in my job in years and would spend a few hours at the weekend trying to keepup to speed with the latest technical developments that interested me. Like many during Covid, I took a step back and decided I'd just get m,ore and more frustrated and something needed to change. I did a couple of short courses in Machine Learning in 2020 and 2021 and decided to apply for a full-time Masters. I'd saved enough over the years and could afford it (as long as I. made my own lunch!).

I applied to Imperial College in 2022 but didn't get in - I'm pretty sure they were looking only for those who had a First in their undergrad...... not me!! I had a 2.1 - I like to think that those who got a 2.1 did Uni prpoerly - enjoying their time in college without hitting the books too hard - a nice balance :-). Not one to give up, I tried again in 2023 and to my shock I was accepted to UCL's MSc in Data Science and Machine Learning! I had 6 weeks to commit and decided to take the plunge........
