## The Mystery

I've been going to lectures for two months now, and I have a question:

**Who exactly are these professors talking to?**

Because it's definitely not me.

## The Evidence

### Exhibit A: The Pace

**Professor**: "This is straightforward."
*Writes three blackboards of equations in 45 seconds*

**Professor**: "As you can see..."

**Me**: *Still copying the first line*

### Exhibit B: The Assumptions

**Professor**: "Trivially, we can derive..."
*Skips 17 steps*

**Professor**: "And obviously..."
*Skips 23 more steps*

**Professor**: "Therefore..."
*Arrives at a conclusion I didn't see coming*

### Exhibit C: The Questions

**Professor**: "Any questions?"

**That One Student**: "Yes, in the proof you just showed, could we use a different approach via measure theory?"

**Professor**: *Excited* "Oh, excellent question! Yes, if we consider..."

**Me**: *Didn't understand the original proof, definitely won't understand the alternative*

## The Suspects

I have theories about who the professors are actually lecturing to:

### Theory 1: Their Past Selves
These lectures are what they wish they had heard as students. Unfortunately, past-them had already studied this for 5 years.

### Theory 2: The Three Math Geniuses in the Front Row
There are always three people nodding enthusiastically and taking minimal notes. Maybe lectures are just for them and the rest of us are tourist observers.

### Theory 3: Other Professors
Maybe this is just how academics talk to each other and they forgot we're not all professors?

### Theory 4: No One
Perhaps lecturing is just a form of academic performance art and comprehension was never the goal.

## The Lecture Experience

**0:00** - Lecture starts, I'm ready
**0:05** - Following along nicely
**0:10** - Wait, when did we get to this topic?
**0:15** - Completely lost
**0:20** - Accepting my fate
**0:45** - Existential crisis
**0:50** - Professor: "We'll continue this next time"
**0:51** - *There's MORE??*

## Different Student Responses

I've observed my classmates have different coping strategies:

**The Note-Takers**: Writing everything down frantically, will understand it later (optimistic)

**The Recorders**: Recording the lecture, will definitely watch it later (lying to themselves)

**The Questioners**: Actually stopping the professor to ask questions (brave souls)

**The Acceptors**: Just vibing with the confusion (me)

## The Translation

What I hear vs. What they mean:

**"Clearly..."** = This took me 3 hours to figure out

**"Simply..."** = Haha, nothing is simple

**"Intuitively..."** = Your intuition is wrong, trust the math

**"It follows that..."** = *significant leap of logic ahead*

**"Without loss of generality..."** = I'm skipping something important

**"Left as an exercise for the reader"** = I don't feel like explaining this

## The Twist

Plot twist: I've started understanding the lectures. Not all of them. Not even most of them. But sometimes, SOMETIMES, I follow the whole thing.

And when that happens? It's magical.

Turns out they might have been talking to me after all. I just needed to learn the language first.

## The Revelation

The lectures aren't for present-me. They're for future-me.

Present-me: Confused, taking notes, hoping for the best
Future-me: *Reads notes from three weeks ago* "Oh, THAT'S what they meant!"

It's a delayed-understanding delivery system.

## The Conclusion

Who are they talking to?

They're talking to the person I'll be by the end of this program.

Current-me is just sitting in to take notes for Future-me.

*Current-me would like to file a complaint with Future-me about this arrangement...*
