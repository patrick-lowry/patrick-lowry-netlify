## The One Month Mark

It's been a month. A full month of:
- Feeling confused
- Pretending I understand
- Actually understanding sometimes!
- Immediately getting confused again

It's time to assess the situation realistically.

## What I Thought Would Happen

**Week 1**: Bit of a struggle, but I'll catch up
**Week 4**: Feeling comfortable with the material
**Week 8**: Thriving
**Week 12**: Basically a machine learning expert

## What Actually Happened

**Week 1**: Drowning
**Week 2**: Still drowning, but with better technique
**Week 3**: Occasionally treading water
**Week 4**: Accepting that drowning might be my baseline

## Setting Realistic Goals

### Original Goal
*"I want to graduate top of my class and get a job at Google DeepMind."*

### Revised Goal
*"I want to graduate."*

### Even More Realistic Goal
*"I want to understand what I'm doing at least 30% of the time."*

### Most Realistic Goal
*"I want to stop having nightmares about matrices."*

## The Reality Check

I've had to accept some truths:

1. **I will not be top of the class**
   That position is reserved for the people who read textbooks "for fun"

2. **I will not understand everything**
   Some things are just going to whoosh over my head like a mathematical fighter jet

3. **I will have to work twice as hard**
   While the math graduates are conceptually understanding the lectures, I'm still trying to figure out what sigma means

4. **That's okay**
   This is the hard part to accept

## The Upside of Being Behind

Being the person who struggles means:

- When I do get something, I REALLY get it (had to fight for that knowledge)
- I ask the "dumb" questions that others are too afraid to ask
- My notes are extremely detailed (out of necessity)
- I deeply appreciate any small victory

## The Plan Going Forward

1. **Stop comparing myself to the Cambridge math graduates**
   They had a 3-year head start, it's not a fair comparison

2. **Celebrate small wins**
   Understood a proof? That's a win.
   Didn't cry during lecture? Also a win.

3. **Focus on fundamentals**
   Maybe I won't understand the cutting-edge research, but I can understand the basics really well

4. **Remember why I'm here**
   To learn machine learning, not to prove I'm the smartest person in the room (spoiler: I'm not)

## The Mantra

*"I don't need to be the best. I just need to be better than I was yesterday."*

Sometimes yesterday-me understood nothing.
Today-me understands almost nothing.
That's progress!

*One month down, eleven to go. Let's do this.*
